🔥 What's New in Hotlist

Big updates.
Zero friction.

Five improvements to make your movie and TV tracking faster, smarter, and more flexible on any device.

🔓
Open Access

Browse before you sign in.

No more login wall. Open Hotlist and immediately see the Hotlist tab, search results, and title details — without creating an account. Sign in only when you're ready to save something.

⏳
Sessions

Stay signed in for 90 days.

Sessions last three months. Your phone won't ask you to log back in every few days.

90 days between sign-ins
🔍
Smart Search

Finds what you mean, not just what you type.

Search understands articles and word order. "the batman" and "batman" both surface the same film. Recent titles rise to the top automatically.

"the batman" "batman" same result ✓
📦
Archive

Keep it, without cluttering your list.

Swipe right to archive a title — it disappears from your main list but isn't gone. Find it any time under the Archived filter. Swipe right again to bring it back.

Swipe right
Archive title → moves to Archived
Swipe left
Delete title → gone permanently
Type filter → Archived
View all archived titles
☑️
Bulk Select

Manage many titles at once.

Tap the checkbox icon in the toolbar to enter select mode. Pick as many titles as you like, then archive or delete them all in one tap.

  1. 1 Tap the ☑ checkbox icon in the toolbar to enter select mode
  2. 2 Tap cards to select them — a ring appears around each one
  3. 3 Use Select all or pick individually
  4. 4 Tap Archive or Delete in the bottom action bar
  5. 5 Tap Cancel to exit without making changes
